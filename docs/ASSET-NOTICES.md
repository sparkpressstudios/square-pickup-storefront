# Demo asset notices

`client/public/images/demo-product-atlas.png` is original synthetic imagery generated with the built-in imagegen tool for this project. It contains four unbranded synthetic products, no real merchant assets or people. `config/demo-presentation.ts` assigns its panels; merchants can replace presentation without changing commerce code. The full generation prompt follows below.

Locally bundled `@fontsource/poppins@5.3.0` uses OFL-1.1; `@fontsource/roboto-slab@5.3.0` uses Apache-2.0. Their supplied notices are preserved at `/licenses/Poppins-OFL.txt` and `/licenses/Roboto-Slab-Apache-2.txt`. These font notices do not choose a license for the starter project. No original merchant font binary or branded photos/logos are included.

## Generation prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: one 4:3 photographic product atlas for a synthetic retail demo. Create a single image divided into an exact edge-to-edge two-by-two grid of four equal 4:3 panels, no gutters, no borders, no text, no logos or watermarks. Each panel is an independent polished studio product photo with generous negative space and fully contained subject: top left one amber glass candle in a reusable vessel with an unbranded small cream label, muted pale sage background; top right one shallow sand-colored ceramic oval catchall tray, pale warm gray background; bottom left one brass wick trimmer with circular scissor handles, warm light stone background; bottom right one clear jar of golden oat granola with a matte dark lid and blank cream label, pale green background. Camera three-quarter tabletop angle, soft natural side lighting, realistic material texture. No people, no brand marks, no readable writing. Product atlas ideally 1536x1152 with each quadrant independently usable as a card image. Subjects centered in each quadrant and small enough not to touch edges. This is newly generated synthetic food and non-food product photography, not any actual merchant's assets.
